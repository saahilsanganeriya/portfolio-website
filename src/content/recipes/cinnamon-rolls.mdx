## Method

1. **Rub in the butter.** Whisk the flour, sugar and salt in the bowl of a stand mixer, then work in the softened butter with your fingers or two forks until it disappears into the flour.
2. **Make the dough.** Warm the milk to about 100°F (38°C) and whisk in the yeast. With the dough hook on medium-low, add the milk, eggs and yolk, and mix for 2 to 3 minutes, until the dough comes together smooth.
3. **First rise.** Knead by hand for 30 to 60 seconds, set the dough in a greased bowl, cover it and leave it until doubled, about 90 minutes.
4. **Mix the filling.** Whisk the brown sugar, muscovado and cinnamon together.
5. **Roll out.** Punch the dough down and roll it into a long rectangle, about 27 inches (69 cm) by ¼ inch (6 mm) thick, with a long edge toward you. Spread the butter over all of it, scatter the cinnamon sugar evenly and pat it in lightly.
6. **Roll up and cut.** Roll the dough up tightly from the near edge into a log, and cut it into 12 rolls, each 2 inches (5 cm) thick, with a serrated knife.
7. **Second rise.** Butter a 9-by-13-inch (23 by 30 cm) dish and set the rolls in 4 rows of 3, each tail facing its neighbour. Cover and let them grow to about one and a half times their size, 30 to 45 minutes.
8. **Bake.** Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until golden. Let them cool in the dish for 15 minutes.
9. **Glaze.** Beat the cream cheese smooth, then the powdered sugar into it. Whisk in the milk until the glaze is thick but still falls off a spoon, adding more a teaspoon at a time if needed. Stir in the vanilla and pour it over the rolls.

## Notes

- **Source.** Adapted from Joshua Weissman's [Making Cinnabon Cinnamon Rolls At Home | But Better](https://www.joshuaweissman.com/recipes/best-cinnabon-cinnamon-roll-recipe). The quantities, sizes, times and temperatures are his. The method is reworded step for step.
- **Butter into the flour first.** Rubbing the butter in before any liquid coats the flour in fat, which keeps the crumb soft.
- **Packed close.** Rolls that touch in the pan rise into each other and stay soft at the sides.
- **Muscovado is optional.** It adds a darker, molasses edge to the filling.
- **Glaze timing.** The 15 minutes the rolls cool in the dish is enough time to make the glaze.
