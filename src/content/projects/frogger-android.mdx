Frogger is a good first game because the rules fit in one sentence: cross the road, then the river, and do
not get hit or fall in. This version is an Android app in plain Java, with no game engine, and with the four
Ninja Turtles as the characters you can play.

You pick a name, a difficulty and a turtle. Easy gives you 5 lives, medium 3 and hard 1. There are 5 lanes
of traffic at different speeds and 4 river lanes of floating logs. Moving forward scores points, and
crossing the water scores double.

Everything runs in one activity. Timers move the vehicles and logs, and every tick checks each object's
bounds against the player. All of it, from setup to the win and lose screens, sits in a 559-line
`MainActivity.java`.

## What I would change

One 559-line activity was quick to write and hard to change. The game state, the drawing and the loop
belong in separate pieces that can be tested on their own. The turtle artwork also belongs to its owners,
so none of it appears here.
