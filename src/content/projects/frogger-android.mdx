Frogger is a good first game because the rules fit in one sentence: cross the road, then the river, and do
not get hit or fall in. Five of us built this version for CS 2340 at Georgia Tech in spring 2023, over five
sprints. It is an Android app in plain Java, with no game engine, and the four Ninja Turtles are the
characters you can play.

You pick a name, a difficulty and a turtle. Easy gives you 5 lives, medium 3 and hard 1. There are 5 lanes
of traffic at three speeds and 4 river lanes of floating logs. Moving forward scores points, and crossing
the water scores double. A hit costs a life and resets your score.

Everything runs in one activity. Timers move the vehicles and logs, and every vehicle checks its bounds
against the player as it moves. All of it, from setup to the win and lose screens, sits in a 559-line
`MainActivity.java`.

The repository records authors only for the tests. I wrote 8 of the 40, and I put the finished app on
GitHub after the term.

## What I would change

The river never became a hazard. The water check is written but never called, so a turtle can walk
straight across. Our tests mock the activity and assert nothing, so they could not catch a bug. One
559-line activity was also hard to change. The game state, the drawing and the loop belong in separate
pieces that can be tested on their own. The turtle artwork belongs to its owners, so none of it appears
here.
