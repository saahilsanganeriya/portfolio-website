My father moved from Guwahati, in Assam, to Delhi and built his own spinning mill, Sanganeriya Spinning Mills. In a spinning mill, loose fiber is drawn out and twisted until it holds together as thread. Thread is where cloth begins. The thread that runs through this reel starts in his mill.
