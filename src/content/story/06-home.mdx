Delhi is a long way from here, and the question of India or America has not gone away. There is a comforting idea that home is everywhere, because the sky is the same everywhere. I only half believe it. What I do know is why I left: to do work big enough to be worth the distance. The mango plant is on the windowsill now. It grows slowly. It is still growing.
