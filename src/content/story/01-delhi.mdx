I grew up in Delhi, in a house with a garden. When I was in kindergarten, my mother and I planted a mango seed there. Mango plants grow slowly. You can watch one for a week and see nothing change. For years it seemed to keep time with me, growing when I grew and stalling when I stalled. When it died during a renovation of the house, I planted another.
