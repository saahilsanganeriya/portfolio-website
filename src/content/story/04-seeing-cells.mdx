At Georgia Tech I did research on microscope images of cells. A single image can hold hundreds of them, pressed against each other. I helped build SAMCell, a model that finds and outlines each cell without anyone pointing at it, and it held up on a microscope it had never seen. We published it in *PLOS ONE* in 2025.
