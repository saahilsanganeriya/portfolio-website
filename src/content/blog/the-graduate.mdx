> “Ben, what are you doing?”
>
> “Well, I would say that I’m just drifting here in the pool.”

Benjamin Braddock has just finished college and come home to his parents’ house in California with no plan. Mike Nichols’ 1967 film follows his summer: an affair with Mrs. Robinson, a family friend, then a love for her daughter Elaine that he cannot leave alone. It ends on a bus.

I watched it in December 2023, a year before my own graduation, and I was scared of graduating. This film gets that. It understands being confused about your future and passive about it, as if life were something happening *to* you. It understands the fear of ending up like everyone else. When you feel like that, the only way to feel in control is to be reckless and watch things come apart, and Ben does exactly that.

The moment I keep is near the end. Ben and Elaine are on the bus, and for a second his smile fades. When I wrote about it I said I would come back to this film over the years, and that it would comfort me and not comfort me at the same time.

I have graduated twice since. I have not watched it again yet.
