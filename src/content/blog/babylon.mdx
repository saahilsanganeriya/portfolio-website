*Babylon* is Damien Chazelle’s film about Hollywood at the end of the 1920s, when silent pictures gave way to sound and a lot of the people who had made them were left behind. It follows an unknown actress and a young man desperate to work in pictures as they rise, while a silent star watches his time run out.

I saw it on 70mm film on New Year’s Day 2023. If I had more stars to give, it would have gotten all of them. That night it outranked films in my top five or ten, which a few hours earlier I would have said was impossible. I logged it as the greatest movie of all time. It was one of the only out-of-body experiences I have had watching a film.

I went back eleven days later. Then again in July, and again in September. Each of those times, instead of a review, I logged a single line from the film. First “ain’t life grand?” Then “something bigger.” Then “It’s easier to be on my own.”

Five viewings, five stars each. The fifth time, in October 2025, I did not write anything.
