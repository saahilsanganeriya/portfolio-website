*Spotlight* is Tom McCarthy’s 2015 film about the Spotlight team, the *Boston Globe*’s investigative unit. It follows their investigation into the decades-long cover-up of child sexual abuse by priests in the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Boston. The team’s stories won the *Globe* the 2003 Pulitzer Prize for Public Service. McCarthy wrote the film with Josh Singer, and in February 2016 it won Best Picture and Best Original Screenplay.

I first watched it in March 2023 and gave it four and a half stars. I watched it again on a Sunday in January 2026, almost three years later, and gave it four and a half stars again. I did not write a review either time.

Since the start of 2023 I have logged 73 rewatches of 57 films. For 53 of them my Letterboxd diary holds at least two ratings to compare. Of those 53, 41 kept the same rating every time and 12 moved: seven ended higher than they started, and five lower. *Spotlight* is one of the 41.
