I love crying at movies, and I used to do it often. *Marriage Story* opens with a list of things one character loves about the other, and one of them is that he cries at movies. That was me.

I still cry at films, but by the spring of 2022 it had become hard. The last time had been more than a year earlier. Then I watched *Rothaniel*.

*Rothaniel* is Jerrod Carmichael’s 2022 stand-up special. He performs in a small club and talks about the secrets his family kept, and the one he kept himself: that he is gay. I cried through most of the last quarter. It hit hardest near the end, when he says “I feel okay,” the same words his mother had said to him on the phone.

When someone uses filmmaking to express something, I think it is usually because they cannot find a better way to say what they feel. It is a need. But a film also gives its maker somewhere to hide. Somewhere behind the work, the filmmaker is always leaning on abstraction. A special like this one is stripped down in a way a film would find very hard to be. He has nowhere to hide.

I watched it again eleven days later and gave it the same four and a half stars.
